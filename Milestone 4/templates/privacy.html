Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. We are committed to protecting your personal data.

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information that you choose to provide while using Digital Twin AI:

  • Account data such as username, email, and securely stored password hashes.
  • Financial data such as income, expenses, savings, and transactions you choose to log.
  • Study data such as study hours, subjects, and productivity scores.
  • Fitness data such as activity types, duration, and calories burned.
  • Goals such as descriptions, target dates, and completion status.
  • Chat messages consisting of questions and responses with the AI assistant.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to provide and improve the personal decision-support experience.

  • Display your personal dashboard and activity summaries.
  • Generate financial, academic, and fitness forecasts.
  • Calculate progress indicators such as savings rate and Life Score.
  • Provide simulations and personalized recommendations.
  • Maintain your account, authentication, and saved preferences.

How We Protect Your Data

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information stored by the application.

  • Passwords are hashed using Werkzeug before storage.
  • Login sessions are managed through Flask-Login.
  • Administrative features are restricted by user roles.
  • Sensitive configuration uses environment variables.
  • Development data is stored locally in SQLite.

AI and Third-Party Services

The optional Gemini-powered assistant may process the information needed to answer your request when its API integration is enabled. The Gemini API key is configured through an environment variable and is not stored in the application interface.

External libraries and content services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Your Choices

You decide which financial, study, fitness, and goal information to record. You can stop using the application at any time. For questions about your data or this policy, contact the project administrator.
