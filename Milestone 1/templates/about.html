Project overview

AI-Based Visual Risk and Compliance Intelligence System

An intelligent personal decision-support system that creates a digital representation of a user's habits, behaviors, financial activities, study patterns, and lifestyle choices.

Project summary

Understand your present. Plan your next move.

The platform leverages AI, Machine Learning, and Predictive Analytics to forecast future outcomes and provide personalized recommendations before users make decisions.

Digital Twin AI combines financial tracking, academic monitoring, fitness logging, goal management, AI-powered predictions, and future simulation in one responsive workspace for individuals and administrators.

From data to decisions

See your current state, review historical patterns, explore possible outcomes, and receive personalized guidance in a single view.

  • CSRF protection for application forms
  • Environment variables for sensitive configuration

What the system measures

Signals that show the bigger picture

Financial

Savings, income, expenses, savings rate, and net savings.

Academic

GPA, study efficiency, total hours, and study streak.

Fitness

Activity, calories burned, daily average, and streak.

Goals

Total goals, achieved goals, and completion rate.

Life Score

A composite health index from 0 to 100% combining finance, academics, fitness, and goals.

Deployment

Ready for local development and production growth

Development uses SQLite, while PostgreSQL can support production deployment. Sensitive values are configured through environment variables.

Environment variables

SECRET_KEY=your-secret-key GEMINI_API_KEY=your-gemini-api-key

The Gemini key is optional; the assistant can use its fallback behavior without it.
